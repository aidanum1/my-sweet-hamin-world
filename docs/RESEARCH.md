Research was done for visual and personality inspiration, not to simulate anyone's life.
Everything in the game is a whimsical, fictional, unofficial fan tribute.

## 2. COMMONLY REPEATED FAN ASSOCIATIONS (not official)

- 🐹 Hamster is the emoji listed on a fan-maintained profile.
- "Bicycle boy" / "Haru" were pre-reveal fan nicknames.
- "Center face" / visual-member framing.
- **Sheep smile** — no public source was found. In this game the sheep is used purely as *our world's* mascot language, as requested by the project brief.
- No verified food preferences were found. **The game makes no claims about what Hamin likes to eat.**

## 3. PURELY FICTIONAL GAME ELEMENTS

- "Sweet Reply High" — a fictional pastel school (no show logos or names of real schools).
- All NPCs (Momo the bunny, Chef Mongmong the sheep, Bori the puppy, etc.).
- The Dog Chase, Dress-Up and Eating games; all foods are generic cafeteria/snack foods.
- "Sweet Sea Beach" — a whimsical seaside inspired by generic Korean harbour-city imagery (red/white lighthouse, boardwalk, pastel sea). It makes no biographical claim.
- A cameo bicycle prop in the schoolyard (nod to the public "bicycle boy" nickname).
- A tiny hamster companion ("Ham-ssi") appears as a friendly NPC, referencing the fan emoji.
- All music is original, synthesised in the browser.

Motifs: pastel cumulus sky, glowing sparkle stars, ✨ ♡ 💕 🎵 💌, rounded cards, float / gentle pulse / sparkle-burst
animations, bouncy easing, "dreamy universe" tone. We translate this into a 3D world: the sky gradient, sparkle
particles, heart currency, the blue→pink gradient on headings and the bouncy UI easing all carry over; the sheep
motif and stationery/sticker language are new additions for the game.

## 5. Content safety decisions

- No real photos, logos (SM, Mnet), or copyrighted songs are used.
- No romance framing, private-life speculation or fake quotes. NPCs speak about "Hamin" only in the context of the game.
- Credits state clearly that the game is unofficial and fan-made.
